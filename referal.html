Frequently asked questions

Know the basics before sharing.

When will I receive my reward?

After the referred student completes the eligible admission process and the academy verifies the referral. Confirm the current terms with RSI Academy management.

Where should my friend enter the code?

Ask your friend to mention your referral code to the admission team when applying or enrolling.

Can I refer more than one friend?

You can share your invitation with friends. Reward eligibility and limits are subject to the academy's current referral policy.

Does this page automatically track referrals?

No. This is a front-end page; it does not send applications or referral records to a server. An admin dashboard and database are needed for verified tracking.
